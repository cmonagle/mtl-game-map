# 03 — The hider deck: cards & curses

> ⚠️ **Unofficial summary — [official rules](https://rules.jetlagthegame.com/) are authoritative.**
> Card effects and the curse list below are summarised from community/rules mirrors and may not
> match the current printed deck. Treat the rulebook (and your own house rules) as the source of truth.

Answering questions is how the hider **earns cards**. The cards are the hider's only defence:
they extend hiding time, dodge questions, and hobble the seekers with curses. This file
covers the deck and the standard curses.

## Card economy

- After every answered question the hider **draws** cards per that question's reward
  (e.g. Radar = `draw 2, keep 1` — draw two, keep one, discard the rest).
- The hider holds a **hand of up to 6 cards** and manages it actively; excess must be
  discarded.
- Cards are **kept until played or discarded**. Time-bonus cards only count if still in hand
  at the end of the round.

> Some app-based trackers layer a *coin / curse-dice* economy on top of this (buy curse dice
> for coins, roll for a random curse). That is a variant — the base tabletop rulebook is
> purely the **draw/keep + discard-to-cast** card system described here. Pick one and be
> consistent.

## Card types

### Time Bonus
Adds time to the hider's final score. Values scale with game size. **Only counts if held at
round end** — discarding one to pay a curse cost forfeits that time. This is the central
tension: spend cards to survive longer, but spending can cost you the very time you're
racing for.

### Powerups (one-time effects)
| Powerup | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Veto** | Play in response to a question *instead of answering* it. |
| **Randomize** | Convert the asked question into a different, un-asked question from the same category. |
| **Discard 1, draw 2** | Card cycling. |
| **Discard 2, draw 3** | Stronger card cycling. |
| **Draw 1, expand hand +1** | Permanently raise hand-size cap by one. |
| **Duplicate** | Copy another card; if kept, doubles a time bonus. |
| **Move** | Pauses the timer and grants time to relocate to a **new** hiding zone. |

### Curses
Cards the hider plays **on the seekers** to slow them down. Each curse has a **casting cost**
— either a resource (discard N cards) or a game condition (e.g. seekers must be a minimum
distance away). See the full list below.

## Curse rules

- A curse's cost may be **discarding cards** and/or a **game condition** gate.
- You may play **multiple curses at once**, **except** you can never have more than **one
  active curse that blocks questions or blocks transit** at a time — wait for a blocking
  curse to expire before playing another blocker. Non-blocking curses can stack freely.
- The creators explicitly encourage **inventing your own curses** suited to your group and
  city — good candidates for Montreal house rules (see
  [`04-montreal-adaptation.md`](04-montreal-adaptation.md)).

## The 24 standard curses

| # | Curse | Effect (summary) |
|---|-------|------------------|
| 1 | **Luxury Car** | Seekers can't ask questions until they find & photograph a luxury car (MSRP $25k+). |
| 2 | **Bridge Troll** | Seekers must get under a bridge before their next question. |
| 3 | **Drained Brain** | Hider discards their hand instead of answering (and gets no reward) to escape a question. |
| 4 | **Water Weight** | Seekers must carry 2 gallons of liquid for the curse's duration. |
| 5 | **Zoologist** | Seekers must photograph a wild insect/creature. |
| 6 | **Egg Partner** | Seekers must keep a real egg intact for the rest of the game. |
| 7 | **Jammed Door** | Seekers roll 2d6 to pass through any doorway; fail = can't enter. |
| 8 | **Spotty Memory** | One random question category becomes unavailable. |
| 9 | **Bird Guide** | Seekers must film a video with a bird visible on camera. |
| 10 | **Unguided Tourist** | Seekers must photograph a structure visible on Google Street View. |
| 11 | **Ransom Note** | Hider asks their next question in letters cut from printed material. |
| 12 | **Mediocre Travel Agent** | Seekers must obtain a souvenir from a public destination. |
| 13 | **Impressionable Consumer** | Seekers must enter/patronise a nearby advertised business. |
| 14 | **U-Turn** | Seekers' next transit stop must move them *further* from the hider. |
| 15 | **Cairn** | Seekers must build a rock stack at their current location. |
| 16 | **Distant Cuisine** | Seekers must dine at a restaurant of a specified foreign cuisine. |
| 17 | **Lemon Phylactery** | Seekers must keep a real lemon attached to skin/clothing. |
| 18 | **Gambler's Feet** | Seekers walk a number of steps set by a d6 roll. |
| 19 | **Hidden Hangman** | Seekers must win a game of hangman (5-letter word) before asking. |
| 20 | **Endless Tumble** | Seekers roll a die and must move 100 ft per pip. |
| 21 | **Right Turn** | Seekers must navigate to a specified right-turn intersection. |
| 22 | **Urban Explorer** | Seekers can't ask questions while at transit stations. |
| 23 | **Overflowing Chalice** | Increases question card draws (e.g. matching becomes "draw 4, keep 1") — a *self-buff* for the hider. |
| 24 | **Labyrinth** | Seekers must solve a hand-drawn maze within a time limit. |

> Curses #1, #7, #19, #22, #24 (and any that stop questioning/transit) are the **blocking**
> curses — only one active at a time.
